## Fixed in 0.3.5–0.3.6 (the original session, verified with window captures)

- Window sizing: `NSHostingController` sized the window to its content (860×1838, taller than the screen). `MainWindowController` sets `sizingOptions = []`, opens at 1060×720, autosaves as "FindMyRingWindow" and keeps a restored frame on screen. Captured at 967×720 on the portrait display.
- Widgets in the dimmed (vibrant) desktop style: white labels on the orange fill vanished (the small Ring widget's button was a blank grey bar). Filled buttons and map pins use `filledFace(_:)`; captured readable ("Ring ⌃⌥⌘R").
- Palette: the hidden title bar left an empty band on top; `.ignoresSafeArea()`. Captured without it, search field focused.
- Offline devices after online ones in the menu, palette (`search`, this session's AppModel work) and the window's grids (`targets(in:)`).
- Bug 2: links carry `?id=` (`RingTarget.link`), and LinkHandler resolves the id first, the name only as a fallback.
- Bug 3: the small Ring widget's `widgetURL` rings an AirTag hero through the app.
- Bug 4: in the kit, a device-list reply without `content` throws `.server` (plus this session's AppModel guard against replacing a saved list with an empty one).
- Bug 5: this session's `showRefreshError`.
- Bug 6: `history.json` writes go through `NSFileCoordinator` (`SharedStore.record` / `clearHistory`).
- Bug 7 (part): "Reload devices" restarts the timer; "Show in Maps" builds its URL with `URLComponents` (`&`/`+` escaped).

## GPT-Astra's review in short

Ranked UX problems: false "Signed in" (fixed), success messages that overclaim, AirTags gated behind iCloud sign-in, the floating empty AirTags page (fixed), no first-run onboarding. Features: stable reorderable favorites across devices and AirTags, local aliases with ID-based links, per-widget targets, a "finding session" (ring again, cancel). Hide the map widget until location freshness is trustworthy. Risks: sign-in volume and persistent bad credentials (fixed in part), unconfirmed links, AirTag automation clicking the wrong thing (fixed: frontmost check, nearest-name card rule, one automation at a time), private-API changes turning into false success, password custody. Icons: A, then C, then B; simplify A's bevels and use a separate monochrome menu-bar glyph.
